The Verge

Android 16 redesigns the notification shade with dynamic grouping

Dieter BohnMay 31, 2026 · 6 min read

Google is overhauling the notification system in Android 16 with "dynamic grouping" — an AI-powered feature that clusters related notifications across apps into unified conversation threads.

Notifications from the same conversation thread (email reply, Slack message, SMS) are now grouped into a single expandable card, regardless of the source app.

The system uses on-device language models to identify conversational context, ensuring that related messages aren't lost in the noise of unrelated alerts. Users can train the grouping by manually splitting or merging notification clusters.

Developer Preview 3 ships this week with the feature enabled by default on Pixel. OEM adoption is expected by Q4 2026.
